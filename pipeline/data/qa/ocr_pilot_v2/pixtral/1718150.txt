Is this creature real?

Last night, June 16th, 2004, multiple Coldwater residents reported a creature sighting in the woods off of Route 75. The creature, described as a large, black, shadowy figure with glowing red eyes, was seen by a group of teenagers who were out past the town's curfew. The creature reportedly chased the teenagers before disappearing into the woods.

The Coldwater Police Department has issued a statement urging residents to stay indoors and avoid the woods until further notice. The police are currently investigating the sightings and have not yet determined if the creature is real or a hoax.

In the meantime, the town of Coldwater has implemented a curfew from 9:00 PM until further notice. Residents are advised to stay indoors and avoid the woods during this time.
